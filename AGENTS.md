# Project preferences

- Keep product copy in Vietnamese and preserve STAR navy/gold branding.
- Keep join, creation, waiting and race routes within the viewport. Long lists and dialogs may scroll internally; do not hide required controls to achieve this.
- Use descriptive commit subjects that explain the change. Never use the generic `Update Site source` message.
- When publishing through a helper with a fixed generic commit message, create a descriptive commit through its ordered commands before its source-save step.
