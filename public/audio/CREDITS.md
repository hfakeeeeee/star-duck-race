# Playful toy-duck calls

`quack-boing.wav`, `quack-giggle.wav` and `quack-squeaky.wav` are original
procedural cartoon duck sounds created for this project, with no external samples.
They use moving nasal formants, springy pitch bends and short double calls.

Regenerate with `python scripts/make-playful-quacks.py`.
Format: mono, 32 kHz, 16-bit PCM WAV, peak normalized to 78%.
