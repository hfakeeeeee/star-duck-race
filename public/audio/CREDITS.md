# Recorded duck quacks

All three source recordings are CC0 1.0. License and source pages verified on
2026-11-15. These replace the procedural toy sounds with recognizable duck calls.

| Local file | Recording | Creator | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| quack-single.wav | single quack from a duck | Mikes-MultiMedia, extracted from squashy555's ducks1.wav | https://freesound.org/people/Mikes-MultiMedia/sounds/418509/ |
| quack-bright.wav | quack | Mari0411 | https://freesound.org/people/Mari0411/sounds/791152/ |
| quack-chatter.wav | Duck Quack | gibarroule | https://freesound.org/people/gibarroule/sounds/365133/ |

First recording's original: https://freesound.org/people/squashy555/sounds/244329/
License: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Local processing: short excerpts, mono 32 kHz / 16-bit PCM, DC removal, short
edge fades and peak normalization to 78%. No synthesized tones, pitch shifting
or added effects. The chatter excerpt retains two consecutive natural calls.
Regenerate with `python scripts/prepare-quacks.py` (requires ffmpeg).
